MATH TUNES

Tunes and live graphs generated from math functions.

Helpers you can use in expressions

All of Math (sin, cos, sqrt, floor, log2, PI, …) plus:

  • seq(t, rate, list) – the list item playing at time t, stepping rate times per minute (loops)
  • env(t, rate, decay) – a pluck that restarts rate times per minute and fades with time constant decay
  • scale(degree, root, MAJOR | MINOR | PENTA) – frequency of a scale degree
  • range(a, b), FIB, PI_DIGITS, PHI, collatz(n), logistic(r, x0, count)

Pitch math: one octave = ×2, one semitone = ×21/12, the tritone = ×√2.
